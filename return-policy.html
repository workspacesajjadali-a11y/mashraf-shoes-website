Return & Exchange Policy

We want you to receive exactly what you ordered. If we make a mistake with your order, we will make it right.

If We Sent the Wrong Item

If you receive a wrong size, wrong design, wrong colour, or wrong item because of a mistake from M Ashraf Shoes, we will accept the return.

  • The item can be returned to us.
  • We will provide a refund for the returned item.
  • You will not be responsible for the return cost when the mistake is ours.

If You Received What You Ordered

If you receive the exact item, design, colour and size that you ordered, we do not accept a return simply because you changed your mind.

Please carefully check your size, colour and design before placing your order.

Size Exchange

If you received the correct item but later want to change the size—for example, you ordered size 9 but now want size 10—we can provide a size exchange.

  • The customer pays the courier/delivery cost for sending the original pair back to us.
  • M Ashraf Shoes pays the courier/delivery cost for sending the replacement pair to the customer.
  • The replacement pair will be sent after we receive and check the returned pair.

Condition of Returned Shoes

Shoes being returned for an exchange must be in the same condition in which they were received.

  • Do not wear the shoes outdoors.
  • Do not wash or damage the shoes.
  • Do not alter or modify the shoes.
  • Please return them with their original packaging whenever possible.

How to Request a Return or Exchange

  • Contact M Ashraf Shoes with your order details.
  • Tell us whether you received the wrong item or want a size exchange.
  • Send clear photos of the shoes if requested.
  • We will guide you through the return process.
  • For an exchange, the replacement will be sent after the returned pair is received and checked.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is M Ashraf Shoes' return policy?

We accept returns when we make a mistake, such as sending the wrong item, wrong size, wrong design or wrong colour.

Can I return shoes if M Ashraf Shoes sent the wrong size?

Yes. If you ordered one size and we sent a different size, you can return the shoes and receive a refund according to our return policy.

Can I exchange my shoe size?

Yes. If you received the correct shoes but want a different size, you can request an exchange. You pay the delivery cost for sending the original pair back to us, and M Ashraf Shoes pays the delivery cost for sending the replacement pair to you.

Can I return shoes if I simply changed my mind?

No. If you received exactly what you ordered, including the correct item, design, colour and size, we do not accept a return simply because you changed your mind.

Does M Ashraf Shoes sell shoes online in Pakistan?

Yes. M Ashraf Shoes is an online shoe shop in Pakistan offering shoes for customers who want to buy shoes online and have them delivered across Pakistan.

Do you deliver shoes to Islamabad, Rawalpindi and Taxila?

M Ashraf Shoes serves customers across Pakistan, including Taxila, Rawalpindi, Islamabad, Lahore, Peshawar, Faisalabad, Multan, Karachi and other cities.

Can I exchange shoes if I ordered online?

Yes. Online customers can request a size exchange when the shoes are in suitable original condition. Please contact us with your order details before sending anything back.
